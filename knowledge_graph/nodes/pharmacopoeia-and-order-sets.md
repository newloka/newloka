## Facts
- Core Pharmacopoeia Subsystem: Dedicated Rust module `newloka_core::pharmacopoeia` providing structured models, compile-time embedded pharmaceutical monograph storage, multi-key search, brand-to-generic equivalence mapping, and clinical drug safety verification. [valid_from: 2026-11-15, recorded: 2026-11-15, source: newloka_core/src/pharmacopoeia/mod.rs & newloka_core/src/pharmacopoeia/models.rs]
- Official IP 2022 & NLEM Alignment: Bundles verified clinical monographs aligned with the Indian Pharmacopoeia (IP 2022 9th Edition), National Formulary of India (NFI), and National List of Essential Medicines (NLEM 2022), complete with ABDM NRCeS SNOMED-CT clinical concept codes and CDSCO drug regulatory schedules (Schedule H, Schedule H1, Schedule X, Narcotic / NDPS). [valid_from: 2026-11-15, recorded: 2026-11-15, source: newloka_core/src/pharmacopoeia/data/indian_pharmacopoeia.json]
- High-Performance Embedded Index: Compiles monograph datasets directly into the binary via `include_str!`, instantiating zero-allocation Trie-like inverted hash indexes (`PharmacopoeiaIndex`) achieving sub-millisecond search latencies across generic names, official IP titles, therapeutic categories, and commercial Indian trade names. [valid_from: 2026-11-15, recorded: 2026-11-15, source: newloka_core/src/pharmacopoeia/index.rs]
- Indian Commercial Brand-to-Generic Auto-Resolution: Maps prominent Indian commercial brands (e.g., Dolo 650, Calpol, Augmentin, Monocef, Pan 40, Ciplox, Azithral) directly to their primary INN/IP active generic monograph, providing instant dose, strength, route, and frequency prefilling in outpatient prescription pads and inpatient order sets. [valid_from: 2026-11-15, recorded: 2026-11-15, source: newloka_core/src/pharmacopoeia/index.rs & newloka_server/src/rxpad.rs]
- Evidence-Based Inpatient Order Sets: Encodes 10 standard clinical inpatient admission and resuscitation protocols (Acute Coronary Syndrome, Sepsis Hour-1 Bundle, Acute Decompensated Heart Failure, Community-Acquired Pneumonia, Diabetic Ketoacidosis, Acute Ischemic Stroke, Acute Upper GI Bleeding, Routine Post-Operative Care, Acute Exacerbation of COPD, General Medical Admission) with standardized medications, diagnostic labs, monitoring orders, and nursing directives. [valid_from: 2026-11-15, recorded: 2026-11-15, source: newloka_core/src/pharmacopoeia/data/indian_pharmacopoeia.json & newloka_server/src/lib.rs]
- Multi-Axis Drug Safety Shield: Real-time clinical safety screening engine evaluating candidate orders against documented patient allergies (including penicillin/beta-lactam cross-reactivity warnings for cephalosporins), duplicate therapeutic class risks (e.g., co-prescribed systemic NSAIDs), and regulatory prescription safeguards (Schedule H1 register logging, Schedule X double-lock storage). [valid_from: 2026-11-15, recorded: 2026-11-15, source: newloka_core/src/pharmacopoeia/index.rs & newloka_server/src/lib.rs]
- Full-Stack Web Integration: Exposed through comprehensive REST endpoints (`/api/pharmacopoeia/info`, `/api/pharmacopoeia/search`, `/api/pharmacopoeia/entry/:id`, `/api/pharmacopoeia/categories`, `/api/pharmacopoeia/order-sets`, `/api/pharmacopoeia/check-safety`), integrated with client API helpers in `newloka_web/js/api.js`, dynamic CPOE order sets modal and quick-action protocol buttons in `newloka_web/js/app.js`, and dedicated `views.pharmacopoeia` browser tab. [valid_from: 2026-11-15, recorded: 2026-11-15, source: newloka_server/src/lib.rs & newloka_web/js/app.js & newloka_web/js/api.js]
